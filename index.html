1. Event Details

The sponsorship is in support of APEX PITCH & POP 2026, a student-led inter-college celebration of creativity, innovation, media, entertainment, and collaboration organized by Apex Media and Marketing Club, scheduled to be held at Apex College in [Insert Event Dates].

2. Sponsee Obligations

The Sponsee agrees to provide the following deliverables to the Sponsor based on the agreed sponsorship tier:

  • Inclusion of Sponsor's logo and name in all major event promotional materials (posters, banners, backdrops, digital invites, and social media).
  • Stage and venue branding with Sponsor-provided materials (standees, flex boards, flags, etc.).
  • Special mention and verbal acknowledgment during opening, closing, and major event segments.
  • Customized promotional booth space (for Platinum or Gold sponsors) at the venue for engagement.
  • Opportunity to distribute samples or merchandise during the event.
  • Feature in media coverages, including press releases and college digital platforms.
  • Tagging and mentions on the event's official social media handles and campaign hashtags.
  • Any exclusive benefit as agreed upon in the sponsorship proposal.

Note: Detailed deliverables shall be based on the agreed category and sponsorship amount.

3. Sponsor Obligations

The Sponsor agrees to provide the following to support the APEX PITCH & POP 2026:

  • Sponsorship amount of NPR [Insert Agreed Amount in NPR] or in-kind support equivalent to the selected sponsorship tier.
  • Timely submission of company logos, brand guidelines, and promotional materials for use.
  • Delivery of any gift items, vouchers, technical products, or merchandise, as agreed, by [Insert Deadline Date].
  • Coordination with the student organizing team regarding brand usage and representation.
