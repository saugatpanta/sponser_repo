SPONSORSHIP AGREEMENT

Date:  [Insert Date of Agreement]

This Sponsorship Agreement ("Agreement") is made on this [Insert Date], by and between:

APEX MEDIA AND MARKETING CLUB, hereinafter referred to as the "Sponsee," a student-led organizing body under Apex College, located in Mid-Baneshwor, Kathmandu, Nepal,

AND

[Insert Sponsor Company Name], hereinafter referred to as the "Sponsor," located at [Insert Sponsor's Address].

Sponsorship Tier:  [Select from sidebar]

1. Event Details

The sponsorship is in support of APEX PITCH & POP 2026, a student-led inter-college celebration of creativity, innovation, media, entertainment, and collaboration organized by Apex Media and Marketing Club, scheduled to be held at Apex College in [Insert Event Dates].

2. Sponsee Obligations

The Sponsee agrees to provide the following deliverables to the Sponsor based on the agreed sponsorship tier (Platinum, Gold, Silver, Bronze, Stall, Gift, Technical, Merchandise, or Other):

  • Inclusion of Sponsor's logo and name in all major event promotional materials (posters, banners, backdrops, digital invites, and social media).
  • Stage and venue branding with Sponsor-provided materials (standees, flex boards, flags, etc.).
  • Special mention and verbal acknowledgment during opening, closing, and major event segments.
  • Customized promotional booth space (for Platinum or Gold sponsors) at the venue for engagement.
  • Opportunity to distribute samples or merchandise during the event.
  • Feature in media coverages, including press releases and college digital platforms.
  • Tagging and mentions on the event's official social media handles and campaign hashtags.
  • Any exclusive benefit as agreed upon in the sponsorship proposal.

Note: Detailed deliverables shall be based on the agreed category and sponsorship amount.

3. Sponsor Obligations

The Sponsor agrees to provide the following to support the APEX PITCH & POP 2026:

  • Sponsorship amount of NPR [Insert Agreed Amount in NPR] or in-kind support equivalent to the selected sponsorship tier.
  • Timely submission of company logos, brand guidelines, and promotional materials for use.
  • Delivery of any gift items, vouchers, technical products, or merchandise, as agreed, by [Insert Deadline Date].
  • Coordination with the student organizing team regarding brand usage and representation.

4. Sponsor Trademarks and Materials

Subject to this Agreement, the Sponsor grants the Sponsee non-exclusive rights to use Sponsor's name, logos, trademarks, slogans, and relevant content strictly for promotional and marketing purposes of the APEX PITCH & POP 2026. The Sponsee agrees to adhere to the Sponsor's brand guidelines and shall not alter or misuse the brand in any form.

5. Term and Validity

This Agreement shall be valid from the date of signing until the completion of all obligations by both parties and the conclusion of the APEX PITCH & POP 2026.

6. General Conditions
  • This Agreement does not constitute a partnership or joint venture.
  • Any amendments must be made in writing and agreed upon by both parties.
  • In case of event cancellation, both parties shall meet to discuss fair and appropriate next steps or compensation.
  • The Sponsee shall not be held responsible for any unforeseen events (e.g., natural disasters, political unrest) that may affect the event execution.
