Date: 

This Sponsorship Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into on , by and between:

APEX MEDIA AND MARKETING CLUB, hereinafter referred to as the "Sponsee," a student-led organizing body under Apex College, located in Mid-Baneshwor, Kathmandu, Nepal,

AND

[Insert Sponsor Company Name], hereinafter referred to as the "Sponsor," located at [Insert Sponsor's Address].

1.Event Details

This Agreement pertains to the APEX PITCH & POP 2026, a premier student-led inter-college festival celebrating creativity, innovation, media, entertainment, and entrepreneurship, organized by Apex Media and Marketing Club. The event is scheduled to be held at Apex College from June 17–19, 2026.

2.Sponsee Obligations

In consideration of the Sponsor's support, the Sponsee agrees to provide the following benefits and deliverables:

  • Inclusion of the Sponsor's logo and brand name across all major event promotional materials, including posters, banners, backdrops, digital invitations, and official social media posts.
  • Stage and venue branding using Sponsor-provided materials such as standees, flex boards, and display flags, placed in high-visibility zones.
  • Special acknowledgment and verbal mention of the Sponsor during the event's opening ceremony, closing ceremony, and all major segments.
  • Feature in all official media coverage, including press releases and the college's digital platforms.
  • Tagging, mentions, and brand visibility on the event's official social media handles and campaign hashtags throughout the promotional period.
  • Any additional exclusive benefit as mutually agreed upon and documented in the sponsorship proposal.

Note: The specific scope of deliverables may be adjusted based on the agreed sponsorship category and final contribution amount.

3.Sponsor Obligations

The Sponsor agrees to fulfill the following obligations in support of APEX PITCH & POP 2026:

  • A sponsorship contribution of NPR [Insert Agreed Amount], as per the selected sponsorship tier, payable prior to the event date.
  • Timely submission of high-resolution company logos, brand guidelines, and all promotional materials required for event production.
  • Delivery of any gift items, promotional merchandise, or technical products as agreed.
  • Active coordination with the student organizing team with respect to brand usage, representation standards, and material deployment.
